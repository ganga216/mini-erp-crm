1 What is this Project?

This project is an internal management portal built for wholesale and distribution businesses. It helps companies easily manage customers, track product inventory, handle sales delivery challans, and follow up with leads in one clean interface.

3 How the Core Business Flow Works

The system enforces real-world inventory dispatches with database accuracy:

1

Draft Challan

Sales creates a draft delivery challan (`CH-0001`)

2

Price Snapshot

System locks price & SKU at the time of creation

3

Stock Check

Stock is checked before confirming delivery

4

Auto Deduct

Stock is atomically reduced upon confirmation

4 Core Modules Included
  • Customer CRM Module: Search & filter customers (Lead/Active/Inactive, Retail/Wholesale/Distributor), view detail pages, and add follow-up notes.
  • Product & Inventory Module: Manage catalog items, track minimum stock alerts (⚠ red indicators), and view stock IN/OUT audit logs.
  • Sales Challan Module: Multi-step challan builder, draft saving, automatic stock deduction on confirmation, and stock restoration on cancellation.
  • PDF Delivery Challan Export: Clean printable delivery notes via browser print (`Ctrl + P`).
